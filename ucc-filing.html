UCC Filing for Business & Personal Matters

Preparation and filing support for UCC records, searches, corrections, amendments, and due diligence for businesses, lenders, investors, buyers, debtors, and secured parties.

01

UCC-1 & UCC-3 filings

Preparation and submission support across all 50 states. Filing-office requirements and timing vary by jurisdiction.

02

Searches & lien due diligence

UCC search support to help lenders, investors, buyers, and businesses organize relevant filing information.

03

Corrections & changes

UCC-5 correction statement assistance, plus continuation, amendment, and termination filing preparation.

04

Digital-asset collateral

Discuss filing preparation related to crypto or tokenized assets. The applicable law and filing approach depend on the asset and jurisdiction.

05

Portfolio review

UCC filing audits and portfolio cleanup to identify records that may need attention.

06

Representation & priority strategy

Ask whether attorney-led debtor or secured-party representation, perfection strategy, and lien-priority analysis are available for your matter. A filing challenge may require a state-specific petition to the Secretary of State; eligibility and outcome depend on governing law.

i
Important to know

Filing acceptance, perfection, lien priority, and correction or removal outcomes depend on the facts and governing state law. Confirm whether attorney services are part of your engagement.
