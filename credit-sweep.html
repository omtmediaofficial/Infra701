01 / Credit Repair / Credit Sweep

Credit Report Review & Dispute Support

Credit-report review and dispute support for information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable. No service can promise to remove accurate, current information or guarantee a score increase.

Credit report reviewInaccurate informationDispute preparationNo score promises
01

Review your reports

Identify entries you believe are incorrect or incomplete and gather the records related to those items.

02

Organize a truthful dispute

Prepare a clear, fact-based dispute for the relevant reporting company or furnisher. Do not dispute information you know is accurate or submit false identity-theft claims.

03

Understand your options

You may dispute errors directly with credit bureaus and furnishers. Ask for the service contract, fees, cancellation terms, and timing before paying for assistance.

i
Important to know

Credit-repair services cannot legally promise to remove accurate, current negative information. Ask about your rights and costs before signing. The FTC explains that consumers can dispute credit-report errors themselves at no cost.
