02 / The firm

Different service areas.
Clear responsibilities.

INFRA701 LEGAL brings together seven areas of support: attorney-assisted immigration, UCC filing, aged shelf corporations and entity filings, business and personal tax preparation, consumer and commercial data furnishing, credit-report review, and government-contracting preparation.

The professional involved depends on the service. Confirm attorney participation, CPA credentials, deliverables, fees, and written engagement terms directly with the office before work begins.

Every matter is different. Filing offices, taxing authorities, credit bureaus, lenders, and contracting agencies make their own decisions; no service can promise a specific result.
