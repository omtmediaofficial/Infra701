# Infrastructure 701 Legal

A responsive, multi-page static website for INFRA701 LEGAL. The service architecture now covers seven areas: attorney-assisted immigration, UCC filing, aged shelf corporations, business and personal tax preparation, consumer and commercial data furnishing, credit-report review, and government-contracting preparation.

The site uses the supplied neon-sign branding, a custom generated consultation image, an amber-on-charcoal visual system, responsive navigation, motion graphics, and reduced-motion support.

## Build

Requires Node.js 18 or newer. From this folder, run:

```powershell
node build.mjs
```

The build creates the HTML pages, `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt` in the repository root. No package installation is required. The site can be hosted from the repository root on a static host.

## Confirm before launch

- Verify the names, bar jurisdictions, and engagement scope for the attorneys assisting with immigration and UCC matters.
- Verify the CPA's current license, Quick CPA & Consulting firm details, the audit-support offer, and its written terms.
- Confirm shelf-entity availability, state coverage, delivery timing, transfer records, and mail/privacy service availability.
- Confirm the UCC service scope, including legal opinions, representation, priority analysis, and any filing-removal petitions.
- Confirm the data-furnisher platforms and operational services offered for consumer and commercial reporting.
- Review credit-repair fees, written contracts, cancellation disclosures, timing, and state-specific requirements. The site makes no promise to remove accurate information or increase a score.
- Confirm Government Contracting Preparation deliverables and service limits.

The prior immigration intake and asylum forms were removed because the new service architecture no longer uses those resources. The Credit Repair / Credit Sweep page is limited to truthful review and dispute support for information believed inaccurate or incomplete; it does not promise removal of accurate, current information.
