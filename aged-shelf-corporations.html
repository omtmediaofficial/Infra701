Aged corporations & LLCs

Ask about current inventory, entity age, state, status, and transfer documents before selecting an entity. Availability and timing vary.

02

New & foreign filings

New entity formations and foreign qualification filings are offered across all 50 states. State requirements, fees, and processing times differ.

03

C corporation

A corporation is a separate legal entity. Formation, governance, ownership, and tax considerations depend on the state and business plan.

04

S corporation election

S corporation status is a federal tax election for eligible entities and owners, with IRS requirements. Confirm eligibility with a CPA or tax attorney.

05

Limited liability company

LLC requirements, management, and tax treatment depend on the formation state and tax elections. Review the structure with an attorney or CPA.

06

Mail & privacy options

Mail services and privacy options are available in select states. Ask what is available for the jurisdiction you choose.

i
Important to know

An entity’s age alone does not establish operating history, revenue, creditworthiness, or eligibility for financing or contracts. Review its records and status before purchase.
